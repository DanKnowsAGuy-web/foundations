Foundations

A Program of Catechetical Formation

The Light of Christ Illumines All

Foundations is a guided entry into the life of the Orthodox Church, structured around four essential disciplines of Christian formation. It asks for nothing but attention, and offers the slow, unhurried beginning of a work that lasts a lifetime.

About This Program

An Invitation, Not a Funnel

Foundations is offered in the spirit of the ancient catechumenate: a patient walking-alongside, not a course to be completed and set down. There is no obligation here and nothing to sign. You are welcome simply to come, to listen, and to ask. When you are ready, a catechist will meet you where you are and walk the rest of the way with you, at the pace the soul actually keeps.
